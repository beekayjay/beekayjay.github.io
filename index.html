About

I'm a web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, focused on building websites that actually work for the businesses behind them — fast to load, easy to use on any device, and built to turn visitors into customers.

I care about the details that most sites skip: search-engine visibility, structured data, performance, and clear calls-to-action. My client work spans opticians, children's education centres, educational consultancies and tech training hubs — all live in production at their own domains.

Whether it's a hand-coded static site or a WordPress build, my goal is the same: a website that looks professional, ranks in local search, and brings in customers.
