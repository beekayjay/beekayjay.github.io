01 About me

I'm a web developer focused on building websites that actually work for the businesses behind them — fast to load, easy to use on any device, and built to turn visitors into customers. I work with businesses, schools and organisations to bring their services online.

I care about the details that most sites skip: search-engine visibility, structured data, performance, and clear calls-to-action. My client work spans opticians, children's education centres, educational consultancies and tech training hubs — all live in production at their own domains.
